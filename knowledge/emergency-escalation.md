# 🚨 Emergency Escalation SOP

## 1. Critical Lab Values (Red Alert Thresholds)
- Fasting Blood Glucose > 250 mg/dL or < 50 mg/dL
- HbA1c > 10.0%
- Systolic Blood Pressure > 180 mmHg or Diastolic > 120 mmHg

## 2. Mandatory Escalation Steps
1. Immediately flag patient record as HIGH RISK.
2. Trigger Emergency Escalation Workflow.
3. Require Human-in-the-Loop Attending Physician sign-off before patient release or medication change.
