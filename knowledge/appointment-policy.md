# 📅 Hospital Appointment & Scheduling Policy

## 1. Booking Rules
- Routine follow-ups must be scheduled at least 48 hours in advance.
- Urgent consultations must be routed to the Triage Nurse immediately.
- Patient identity must be verified using Patient ID (e.g. P1001) prior to booking confirmation.

## 2. Cancellations & Rescheduling
- Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice require supervisory clearance.
