# 🏥 Patient Discharge Policy & Protocol

## 1. Discharge Readiness Criteria
- Vital signs must remain stable for a minimum of 12 consecutive hours.
- Attending physician must sign the electronic discharge summary.
- Patient medication reconciliations (checking drug interactions and allergies) must be verified.

## 2. Allergy Check Protocol
- If a patient has a documented **Penicillin** or **Sulfa drug** allergy, alternative non-beta-lactam antibiotics must be prescribed.
