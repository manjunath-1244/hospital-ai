# 💊 Medication Administration & Patient Safety Protocol

## 1. High-Alert Medication Double-Check Protocol
- **Independent Double-Check**: High-alert medications (including Insulin, Concentrated Electrolytes, Heparin/Anticoagulants, and Opioids) require an independent double-check by two licensed Registered Nurses prior to administration.
- **Verification Metrics**: Verification includes dosage calculation check, infusion pump rate confirmation, and patient ID validation.

## 2. Patient Identity Verification Protocol
- **Two-Identifier Requirement**: Prior to administering any medication or initiating diagnostic tests, staff must verify the patient using two unique identifiers:
  1. Full Legal Name (e.g. Eleanor Vance)
  2. Medical Record / Patient ID Number (e.g. P1001)
- **Bar-Code Medication Administration (BCMA)**: Scan the patient's wristband barcode and the medication package barcode before administration.

## 3. Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) Reporting Rules
- **Immediate Action**: In the event of an unexpected drug reaction (rash, dyspnea, hypotension, altered mental status), immediately suspend drug administration, maintain IV access with normal saline, and initiate Emergency Triage Protocol.
- **Mandatory Reporting Window**: All adverse drug reactions must be formally logged in the hospital electronic safety registry within **4 hours** of occurrence.
